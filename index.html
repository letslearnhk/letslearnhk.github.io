FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. LetsLearn is free to use. Tutors can choose to swap, teach for free, or agree their own rate with you directly.

When does it launch?

Very soon on Android (Google Play), with iPhone to follow. Join the waitlist and we'll email you on launch day.

Where is it available?

Hong Kong and London first. Online lessons work from anywhere.

Is my location shared?

Never exactly. Listing pins are moved 300–800 m at random, and your device location stays on your phone.
