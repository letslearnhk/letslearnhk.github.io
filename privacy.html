Privacy Policy

13 October 2026

Who we are

LetsLearn ("we", "us") is a skill-sharing app that helps adults in Hong Kong and London find people to teach, learn from, or swap skills with. LetsLearn is operated by Richard Liu, an individual based in Hong Kong, who is responsible for your personal data. For any privacy question or request, contact letslearnhk@gmail.com.

What we collect

• Account details: your email address, display name, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or over) and city.

• Profile content: an optional profile photo, your bio, and the skills you teach and want to learn.

• Listings: title, description, skill, indicative rate, and an approximate area. We never store the exact point you pick. It is moved 300 to 800 m in a random direction before it is saved.

• Messages, session requests (time, length, meeting place or online) and reviews you write or receive.

• Reports and blocks you make, so we can keep the community safe.

• Push notification tokens, if you allow notifications, so we can alert you to new messages.

• Waitlist (our website): if you join it, your email address, city and whether you want to teach or learn. Used only to tell you about the launch and early testing, and deleted on request.

• Device location: used only on your device to centre the map. It is not sent to or stored on our servers.

• Technical data: basic logs (such as IP address, device type and request times) kept by our hosting provider for security.

How we use it

• To run the service: show listings on the map, match swap partners, deliver messages, session requests and notifications, and show reviews (performance of our contract with you).

• To keep users safe: investigate reports, stop abuse and enforce our Terms (legitimate interests).

• To send sign-in codes and essential service emails (performance of our contract with you).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

What other users can see

Other signed-in users can see your display name, profile photo, city, bio, skills, active listings (with their approximate area), and reviews about you. Only the people in a conversation can see its messages and session requests. Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other users.

Who we share it with

We use service providers who process data on our behalf under contract:

• Supabase: database, authentication, photo storage and hosting (servers in London, United Kingdom).

• Google (Gmail): delivering sign-in codes by email.

• Expo: delivering push notifications.

• Apple and Google: map display and push delivery on your device.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

International transfers

Your data is stored in the United Kingdom. If you use LetsLearn in Hong Kong, your data is transferred to and stored in the United Kingdom. Where providers process data outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, messages, session requests, reviews and reports are deleted immediately from our live database, and backups are overwritten within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, and receive a copy of your data. You can edit your profile and delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account). For anything else, contact us at letslearnhk@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (pcpd.org.hk).

Age limit

LetsLearn is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete the account.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.

Language

This policy is available in English and Chinese. If the two versions differ, the English version prevails.

私隱政策

2026年10月13日

關於我們

LetsLearn（「我們」）是一個技能分享應用程式，協助香港及倫敦的成年人尋找可以教授、學習或交換技能的對象。LetsLearn 由居於香港的個人 Richard Liu 營運，並負責處理你的個人資料。如有任何私隱查詢或要求，請聯絡 letslearnhk@gmail.com。

我們收集的資料

• 帳戶資料：電郵地址、顯示名稱、出生日期（用以確認你年滿 18 歲）及所在城市。

• 個人檔案內容：可選的個人照片、自我介紹，以及你可以教授和想學習的技能。

• 刊登內容：標題、描述、技能、參考收費及大約地區。我們從不儲存你所選的確切位置——位置會在儲存前隨機偏移 300 至 800 米。

• 你發出或收到的訊息、上課邀請（時間、時長、見面地點或網上）及評價。

• 你作出的檢舉及封鎖，以維護社群安全。

• 推送通知代碼（如你允許通知），用以通知你新訊息。

• 候補名單（我們的網站）：如你登記，我們會收集你的電郵地址、城市，以及你想教或想學。只會用作通知你上架及搶先測試的消息，你可隨時要求刪除。

• 裝置位置：只在你的裝置上用作地圖定位，不會傳送或儲存到我們的伺服器。

• 技術資料：由我們的託管服務供應商為保安目的保存的基本記錄（例如 IP 地址、裝置類型及請求時間）。

我們如何使用資料

• 提供服務：在地圖上顯示刊登、配對技能交換夥伴、傳送訊息、上課邀請及通知，以及顯示評價（履行我們與你之間的合約）。

• 保障用戶安全：調查檢舉、阻止濫用行為及執行使用條款（正當利益）。

• 發送登入驗證碼及必要的服務電郵（履行合約）。

我們不會出售你的個人資料，亦不會將其用於第三方廣告。

其他用戶可以看到甚麼

其他已登入用戶可以看到你的顯示名稱、個人照片、城市、自我介紹、技能、刊登中的刊登（連同大約地區）及關於你的評價。只有對話中的人可以看到該對話的訊息及上課邀請。你的電郵地址及出生日期絕不會向其他用戶顯示。

我們與誰分享資料

我們使用以下服務供應商，他們根據合約代表我們處理資料：

• Supabase：資料庫、身份驗證、相片儲存及託管（伺服器位於英國倫敦）。

• Google（Gmail）：以電郵發送登入驗證碼。

• Expo：傳送推送通知。

• Apple 及 Google：在你的裝置上顯示地圖及傳送推送通知。

如法律要求或為保障他人安全，我們亦可能披露資料。

跨境轉移

你的資料儲存於英國。如你在香港使用 LetsLearn，你的資料會被轉移至英國儲存。如服務供應商在英國以外地方處理資料，我們會採取適當保障措施，例如英國國際資料轉移協議。

資料保存期限

你的帳戶仍然有效期間，我們會保存你的資料。當你刪除帳戶時，你的個人檔案、刊登、訊息、上課邀請、評價及檢舉會即時從我們的資料庫刪除，備份會在 30 日內被覆寫。伺服器記錄最多保存 90 日。

你的權利

根據英國《通用資料保障條例》及香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》，你可要求查閱、更正或刪除你的個人資料，反對或限制某些處理，以及取得你的資料副本。你可隨時在應用程式內編輯個人檔案及刪除帳戶（我的 → 刪除帳戶）。如有其他要求，請聯絡 letslearnhk@gmail.com。你亦可向英國資訊專員辦公室（ico.org.uk）或香港個人資料私隱專員公署（pcpd.org.hk）投訴。

年齡限制

LetsLearn 只供 18 歲或以上人士使用。我們不會在知情下收集 18 歲以下人士的資料；如發現有此情況，我們會刪除有關帳戶。

政策變更

如本政策有重要變更，我們會在變更生效前於應用程式內通知你。

語言

本政策設有英文及中文版本。如兩者有任何歧異，概以英文版本為準。
